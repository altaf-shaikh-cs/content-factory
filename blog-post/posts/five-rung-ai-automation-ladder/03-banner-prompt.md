## Banner Image Prompt

**Core metaphor:** A ladder rising through layers of soft light, each rung slightly more defined and stable than the one below it, the lower rungs blurred and unfinished, the upper rungs sharp and structural, suggesting sequence and dependency rather than height for its own sake.

**Prompt (universal base):**
A minimal editorial illustration of an abstract ladder rising through a dark gradient void, five rungs, each rung rendered with increasing structural detail and light as it ascends, the lowest rung soft and half-formed, the topmost rung crisp and geometric, no human figure, no screens, no text, atmosphere is quiet and technical rather than dramatic, muted deep blue and charcoal palette with a single warm accent light tracing the ladder's spine, wide banner format, 16:9 ratio, clean negative space, tech editorial cover style

**Tool modifiers:**
- Midjourney: append `--ar 16:9 --style raw --v 6`
- DALL·E / GPT-4o: add "digital illustration, flat design, no gradients"
- Ideogram: add "flat vector illustration, editorial style, tech blog"

**Do NOT include:** code on screens, company logos, text or titles, photorealism

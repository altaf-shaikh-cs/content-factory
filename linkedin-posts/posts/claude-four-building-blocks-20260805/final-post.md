## Final Post

Two engineers, same model, same subscription. One says the AI writes like their team wrote it. The other says it is fine, but you still have to fix everything.

The only difference is configuration.

It is the gap between a new hire on day one and the same new hire after a week of onboarding. Identical person. They can read, reason, write, ship. Day one they still do not know your conventions, your structure, or what not to touch. Capable and useless.

I lived on the wrong side of that for months. Every new chat, I explained the project again, the formatting again, the do-not-touch list again. I was re-onboarding the same hire every morning and calling it a workflow.

Four things close the gap. Most people never turn any of them on.

Skills are your conventions written once, in plain text, picked up automatically whenever a task fits.

Plugins are the whole setup in one install. Skills, connectors, commands, sub-agents, bundled. Someone joins, installs it, and the AI already knows how you work.

Projects are walls between bodies of work, so one context stops leaking into the next.

Artifacts are output that survives the conversation. Documents, decks, tables, small working tools you can hand to someone.

Consistency. Capability. Separation. Output.

None of it needs a better model. It needs the hour of setup you keep postponing.

Pick the workflow that eats your week. Write one Skill for it. Then the next one.

If you have built one custom Skill you actually reuse, tell me what it does. I want to see what other builders automated first.

The model is not your bottleneck. Your setup is.

People keep waiting for the next release to make their AI actually useful. Meanwhile four features that already ship, that you are already paying for, sit untouched.

Skills. Plugins. Projects. Artifacts.

Skills are your standards, written once in plain text, applied automatically every time a task fits. This is the entire reason one person's output looks like their team wrote it and yours looks like a demo.

Plugins are a whole working setup in one install. Skills bundled with connectors, commands, and sub-agents. A new teammate installs it and the AI already knows how you work.

Projects are walls. One body of work per space, no context bleeding from the thing you were doing an hour ago. If anything you touch is confidential, this is not optional.

Artifacts are the output that leaves the chat. Documents, decks, tables, interactive tools, small apps. If everything your AI makes dies inside a conversation, you are using a slice of it.

Consistency, capability, separation, output. Four different problems, four different answers.

None of this needs a better model. It needs an hour of configuration you keep postponing.

Start with one workflow that eats your week. Write one Skill for it.
